# finger-paint

**[Try the finger-paint demo](https://jameskeithharwood.com/finger-paint/)**

Paint in the air with your fingertips. The goal is for each stroke to stay where you drew it as you move the camera.

## First demo

Open the demo on your phone. You can paint directly on the camera view, or show the four-dot marker to anchor a stroke. Dip your index fingertip into the palette at the bottom of the camera view. Make a fist, then open your hand to paint with the selected color. Close your fist to stop. Each stroke uses the marker plane if the marker is visible when you open your fist. Otherwise it stays on the screen. The first test phone is a Samsung Galaxy A15 5G.

Dip into **Erase** at the right end of the palette. Open your fist and sweep the hollow fingertip ring across the paint you want to remove. Close your fist to stop. **Undo** restores the whole sweep. Dip into a color to resume painting.

## Five fingers, five brushes

Dip each fingertip into a virtual color. Paint with a single finger, a few together, or all five in one sweep. Each fingertip keeps its color until dipped again.

## Starting point

[Sentinel Air Strings](https://github.com/jkh2/sentinel-air-strings) demonstrates browser-based fingertip tracking. The first finger-paint prototype will need to combine fingertip tracking with room tracking.
