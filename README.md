# finger-paint

**[Try the finger-paint demo](https://jameskeithharwood.com/finger-paint/)**

Paint in the air with your fingertips. The goal is for each stroke to stay where you drew it as you move the camera.

## First demo

Open the demo on your phone and keep the four-dot marker in view of the front camera. Dip your index fingertip into the palette at the bottom of the camera view. Make a fist, then open your hand to paint with the selected color. Close your fist to stop. The demo anchors strokes to the marker plane. The first test phone is a Samsung Galaxy A15 5G.

## Five fingers, five brushes

Dip each fingertip into a virtual color. Paint with a single finger, a few together, or all five in one sweep. Each fingertip keeps its color until dipped again.

## Starting point

[Sentinel Air Strings](https://github.com/jkh2/sentinel-air-strings) demonstrates browser-based fingertip tracking. The first finger-paint prototype will need to combine fingertip tracking with room tracking.
